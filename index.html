About Me

I'm a fourth year Computer Engineering student at the University of Toronto with a minor in AI. I like building things that solve real problems and I'm most energized when I'm learning something new.

At ONxpress I saw how many moving parts shape large scale construction work and how much pressure teams face navigating regulations and daily workflows. Seeing hundreds of people rely on a tool I built showed me how meaningful well designed software can be in a complex system.

It also helped me see how many gaps exist across different fields and how much I enjoy building tools that make those spaces work better. I want to keep taking on projects that address those gaps so I can learn, grow and make an impact.

Additionally, I'm drawn to big questions about how people and the universe work which is why astronomy, psychology and criminology inspire me. They help me think from different angles and bring creativity into my engineering work. I also love talking about my dog, Shiro.
